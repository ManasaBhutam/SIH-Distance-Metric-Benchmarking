INTERPRETATION GUIDANCE
Silhouette measures cluster separation using each model's own distance matrix. Cluster balance is the smallest cluster size divided by the largest cluster size. ARI measures agreement between the two cluster assignments. A low ARI indicates substantial changes in assignments. These measures should be interpreted together with cluster profiles, missing-data patterns, and the research objective.

Model B still contains employment-related variables such as SALARY, HRSWK, ACTRD and INDCODE. Therefore, removing LFSTAT does not completely remove employment-related information.
